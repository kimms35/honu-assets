## 1. Business models and numbers by company

### Takeaway
The only players that are both large and profitable are (a) supplier-paid subscription/listing businesses with very high gross margins (IndiaMART, Thomasnet, Alibaba.com memberships) and (b) one managed marketplace, Xometry, which needed about 10 years and a high-margin ad business (Thomasnet) before it became Adjusted-EBITDA positive. Heavy managed or "full-stack" models (Zetwerk, Fictiv) reached large revenue but ran thin margins and losses.

### Inferences
- There are three clear money engines. (1) Supplier-paid subscriptions or ads (IndiaMART ~₹72K/supplier/yr; Alibaba ~$4.7K–12.5K/yr; Thomasnet 85–90% GM) are profitable but slow, and limited by churn among low-value suppliers. (2) Managed marketplace as merchant of record (Xometry ~36% GM; Jiga) captures a much larger share of each transaction but needs quoting, QA and working-capital capability. (3) Full-stack contract manufacturer (Zetwerk) has the highest revenue and the thinnest margins.
- For a Korean contract-supplier matching platform, the closest early-stage revenue model is probably the IndiaMART/EMIDAS supplier subscription, with an option to move toward managed transactions in high-value categories (Xometry/Jiga style).

## 2. Managed marketplace vs open directory vs SaaS: what worked in fragmented industrial supply

### Takeaway
Open directories monetized through supplier subscriptions are the proven profitable base in fragmented supply (IndiaMART, Thomasnet, EMIDAS, Alibaba.com). Managed marketplaces create more value per transaction and grow faster, but reach profitability only at scale; Xometry got there around 2025–2026, while Fictiv was sold at a loss-making stage. Pure SaaS does well as an attachment (CADDi, Busy Infotech in IndiaMART), not as the matching layer.

### Cited Findings
- Directory economics: Thomas had an 85% GM in 2021, and Xometry services had an 89.9% GM in Q3 2026, but services grew only about 2%. — [SME](https://www.sme.org/technologies/articles/2021/december/xometry-acquires-thomas/); [Finviz](https://finviz.com/news/414090/xometry-reports-record-third-quarter-2026-results)
- IndiaMART is consistently profitable (₹90.8 crore quarterly PAT) but grows 9–10% with flat or declining paid supplier counts. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/indiamart-q2-profit-rises-10-yoy-to-%E2%82%B991-cr)
- Managed marketplace: Xometry marketplace grew 43% YoY with 36% GM and an 8.5% Adj. EBITDA margin in Q3 2026. — [Finviz](https://finviz.com/news/414090/xometry-reports-record-third-quarter-2026-results); [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001657573/000119312526444354/xmtr-ex99_1.htm)
- Fictiv (managed) had three straight loss years before its $350M exit to MISUMI. — [the-shashi.com](https://the-shashi.com/en/tse/9962/decisions/fictiv-acquisition-2025/)
- Full-stack: Zetwerk had ₹15,913 crore revenue and a ₹1,606 crore loss in FY26, with thin margins. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/zetwerk-makes-its-market-debut-can-scale-alone-widen-its-thin-margins/)
- Hybrid: Protolabs' asset-light network grew about 14% in 2025 while its own factory processes declined. — [3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/323826/3d-printing-financials-protolabs-reports-a-steady-2025-as-digital-manufacturing-and-metal-printing-gain-ground/)

### Inferences
- Xometry's path (managed marketplace, then buying a directory with 500K suppliers and 85% GM) shows the two models complement each other. The directory supplies the supplier base and buyer intent, and the managed layer monetizes repeat, high-value orders.
- In a Korean market of small, fragmented, relationship-driven 뿌리/contract manufacturers, a pure 15–36% take is hard unless the platform owns QA and payment. A supplier subscription plus optional escrow or managed orders is the lower-risk entry.

### Gaps
- There is no clean public take-rate disclosure (GMV vs net revenue) for any industrial matching marketplace other than Xometry's GM proxy.

## 3. Chicken-and-egg, disintermediation (leakage), and trust

### Takeaway
The winners acquired supply cheaply (free listings, government or association channels, acquisition of a directory) and fought leakage by adding value per transaction: instant quoting, merchant-of-record payment, QA, escrow, and zero commission on self-sourced customers. Pure lead-gen models accept leakage and charge a flat subscription instead.

### Cited Findings
- Supply-side bootstrap by acquisition: Xometry bought Thomas's 500K+ suppliers and 1.3M buyers. — [Xometry 8-K 2021](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657573/000119312521350901/d254750dex991.htm)
- Freemium supply funnel: IndiaMART has about 8.9M storefronts but only about 217K payers. EMIDAS free members can list 8 items, Pro-Lite 50, Pro 3,000. — [IndiaMART coverage](https://investmentguruindia.com/newsdetail/indiamart-intermesh-limited-releases-q2fy27-results-reports-consolidated-revenue-rs-429-crore-representing-a-growth-of-10-401459); [Fukui Pref. EMIDAS doc](https://www.pref.fukui.lg.jp/doc/sansei/emidas2026_d/fil/syoudankaisyuttennsha.pdf)
- Anti-leakage by design: Faire charges a 0% commission on Faire Direct (brand-brought) retailers and 15% on marketplace-originated orders, so brands bring their own customers onto the platform. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/faire)
- Leakage accepted: Maker's Row takes "no commission on sales" and charges for access instead; Alibaba and IndiaMART sell memberships and leads. — [Maker's Row help](https://help.makersrow.com/article/204-what-is-makers-row-how-did-you-find-us); [Alibaba KR](https://seller.alibaba.com/kr/usage-guidelines/ggs-membership-benefits-ko)
- Trust tiers: Alibaba's Verified Supplier tier (about $12.5K) includes third-party verification and sits above Gold Supplier. EMIDAS paid tiers give buyer contact details and catalog-download reports. — [Alibaba blog](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/food-beverage/gold-supplier-membership-guide-alibaba-b2b); [Fukui doc](https://www.pref.fukui.lg.jp/doc/sansei/emidas2026_d/fil/syoudankaisyuttennsha.pdf)
- Merchant of record: Jiga reportedly acts as vendor of record with vetted suppliers. — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/ctechnews/article/hj8ti4qxzg)
- Government channels: EMIDAS is promoted through prefectural programs (Fukui) and JETRO materials. — [JETRO NC Network PDF](https://www.jetro.go.jp/ext_images/services/japan_linkage/2024/pdf/NC_network.pdf); [Fukui](https://www.pref.fukui.lg.jp/doc/sansei/emidas2026_d/fil/syoudankaisyuttennsha.pdf)

### Inferences
- In Korea, the analogue to EMIDAS's prefectural channel would be the 지자체, 산업단지공단, 중기부 and KOTRA programs. They can subsidize supplier onboarding and lend trust.
- Escrow or merchant-of-record handling is the main lever against leakage. Without it, the platform should price as a subscription, not a commission.

### Gaps
- There is no quantitative leakage-rate data for any industrial marketplace.

## 4. Failures, down rounds, and lessons

### Takeaway
Most "failures" in this space are down rounds, subscale survival, or fire-sale exits, not outright shutdowns. The common causes are heavy operations with thin margins, low purchase frequency for SMB buyers, and over-funded growth.

### Cited Findings
- Faire: valuation fell from $12.59B (2022) to $5.2B (tender), with 20% layoffs in 2023. It recovered to EBITDA breakeven with about $750M annualized revenue by 2026. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/335b3433-b527-4cc4-acf8-0e10a4942058/); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/02/wholesale-marketplace-faire-survived-fog-of-crazy-valuations-now-eyeing-ipo/)
- Zetwerk: FY25 revenue −11% and FY26 loss ₹1,606 crore, including about ₹1,289 crore of exceptional and impairment charges from exiting civil infrastructure. Lesson: diversifying into low-margin project businesses destroyed value. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/zetwerks-gmv-slips-11-in-fy25-posts-rs-371-cr-loss-10991308); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/zetwerk-makes-its-market-debut-can-scale-alone-widen-its-thin-margins/)
- Fictiv: three loss years, then sold to a strategic buyer (MISUMI) for $350M. — [the-shashi.com](https://the-shashi.com/en/tse/9962/decisions/fictiv-acquisition-2025/)
- MFG.com was sold to Shapeways in 2022. — [Wikipedia](https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/MFG.com)
- Kinnek pivoted from a buyer-side RFQ marketplace to supplier-reputation SaaS. — [Kinnek Medium](https://medium.com/@Kinnek/building-a-router-for-the-b2b-economy-b125fe52726f)
- Generic B2B marketplace failure post-mortems (e.g. Mapudo, a German steel marketplace) are available for further reading. — [Why Mapudo failed](https://medium.com/swlh/why-mapudo-failed-5f3519f1b277)
- Xometry lost money for years before reaching Adj. EBITDA positivity. Q3 2026 Adj. EBITDA improved by $15.3M YoY, implying about $6M in Q3 2025. — [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001657573/000119312526444354/xmtr-ex99_1.htm)

### Inferences
- Lessons: (1) Keep the core matching layer high-margin and capital-light. (2) Add managed services only where QA and payment create real value. (3) Avoid project-type, low-margin diversification. (4) Expect a long road to profitability; Xometry took about 13 years from its 2013 founding.

### Gaps
- Kinnek's shutdown date and causes, and the claimed Xometry GAAP losses by year, could not be verified (page fetches blocked).

## 5. Japanese analogues (CADDi, meviy/MISUMI, NC Network/EMIDAS)

### Takeaway
Japan, the closest structural match to Korea's keiretsu-style supply base, has produced three distinct models: CADDi (AI drawing-data SaaS that started as a managed parts-procurement business), MISUMI meviy (manufacturer-owned instant-quote platform), and NC Network's EMIDAS (long-running supplier-subscription directory with government channels).

### Cited Findings
- CADDi raised a $114M Series D at a $1.2B valuation in Sept 2026 (Dealroom says $110M), for about $234M raised in total. Its prior round was a ~$38M Series C extension led by Atomico at a $470M valuation (Mar 2025). Headcount is about 900, up from 600 in early 2025. Products are CADDi Drawer (renamed CADDi Explorer) and CADDi Agent, and it is expanding into North America. No revenue is disclosed. — [Fortune 2026-09-15](https://fortune.com/2026/09/15/caddi-manufacturing-startup-valuation-funding-round-series-d-exclusive/); [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/16/caddi-raises-114m-at-1-2b-valuation-to-bring-manufacturing-ai-to-north-america/); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/151011-caddi-hits-1-2b-valuation-with-110-million-series-d/); [Fortune 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/03/27/japanese-ai-startup-caddi-venture-capital-funding-atomico-supply-chain-optimization/)
- CADDi began with a B2B supply-chain marketplace for parts, raising an $89M Series C in 2023 for it. — [TechCrunch 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/05/caddi-raises-89m-series-c-to-scale-its-b2b-supply-chain-marketplace-for-manufacturing-parts)
- meviy (MISUMI): 170,000 users in Japan and overseas over 5 years, and a claimed 90% reduction in custom-part procurement time (Apr 2025). ### Inferences
- CADDi's shift from parts marketplace to data/AI SaaS, followed by a $1.2B valuation, suggests that in Japan and Korea style supply chains, software sold to buyers (drawing and cost data) may be valued more highly than pure matching.
- EMIDAS is the most direct template for a Korean early-stage model: a supplier subscription directory, modest scale (22K firms), distributed through local governments.
