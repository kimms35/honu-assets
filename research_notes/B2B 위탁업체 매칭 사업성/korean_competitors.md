# 한국 B2B 위탁/외주 매칭 플랫폼 경쟁 지형 (Korean B2B supplier-matching / outsourcing platforms)

Research method note: Findings come mostly from web search snippets (2026-11-27). Direct page fetches of thevc.kr and magazine.hankyung.com failed (host not reachable from this session), so the paid figures on THE VC and several article bodies could not be checked. DART filings were not reachable directly. Treat single-snippet numbers as "reported", not audited.

## 1. 주요 플랫폼별: 무엇을 매칭하나, 규모, 매출/투자, 수수료 모델, 신뢰 장치

### Takeaway
The market splits into four groups. (a) Manufacturing-process matching: CAPA (machining, 3D printing, injection molding, sheet metal, casting, PCB) and O-SL (apparel). (b) IT/service outsourcing: Wishket, Kmong Enterprise, Soomgo Bizlink. These use escrow and a dedicated manager, and they are the only group with clearly profitable, scaled operators. (c) Large procurement and MRO marketplaces: iMarketKorea, Serveone, MarketBoro. These deal in catalogue goods, not custom work. (d) Logistics: Fassto and Logiket. Packaging-specific matching is thin: Packative is a custom box maker, not a supplier marketplace. Most early-stage manufacturing matchers disclose little public revenue.

**오슬 O-SL (위아더) – 의류 생산 매칭**
- Founded Aug 2019. It offers factory search, comparison quotes, e-contracts, and 3D simulation and patterns for small-batch, reactive apparel production. Self-reported scale is about 1,700 factories and about 6,300 designer members — [넥스트유니콘](https://www.nextunicorn.kr/company/bf91a982fe81a306)

This is a direct-manufacture/print-on-demand model, not open supplier matching — [Packative FAQ](https://packative.com/help/customer-center/frequently-asked); [Packative product](https://packative.com/en/products/paper-twopart/prod_01HWW7R6ZMX7R3P56VCW2XEQEN)

**파스토 Fassto (→ 파스토로보틱스) – 풀필먼트**
- Revenue was 76.4 billion KRW (FY2024). It raised 19 billion KRW in a 2024 Series D against a 50 billion target, with cumulative funding above 130 billion KRW — [디지털데일리](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2024090210093746097); [Dealsite](https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/171431)

An Oct 2026 report says revenue is up more than 50% this year and close to monthly breakeven — [Byline 2026-02](https://byline.network/2026/02/20_fassto/); [Dealsite](https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/171330)

**로지켓 Logiket – 3PL 역경매 매칭**

- Other small players: "모두의물결" (logistics expert matching app) and "로지창고" — [App Store 모두의물결](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/%EB%AA%A8%EB%91%90%EC%9D%98%EB%AC%BC%EA%B2%B0-%EB%AC%BC%EB%A5%98%EC%A0%84%EB%AC%B8%EA%B0%80-%EB%A7%A4%EC%B9%AD%EC%95%B1/id6475091108?uo=4)

**기업 구매 / MRO**
- 아이마켓코리아 FY2025: consolidated revenue about 3.054 trillion KRW (−8%), operating profit 20.3 billion KRW (−54.6%). Mix is about 74% industrial-consumables e-commerce and 23% pharmaceutical wholesale (2Q26) — [Naver 증권 리서치 PDF 2026-05](https://stock.pstatic.net/stock-research/company/40/20260527_company_371400000.pdf); [헤럴드 2026-04](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026042814035255702)
- 서브원: projected to pass 6 trillion KRW in annual revenue in 2026 (projection, not actual), adding affiliates of Hyundai Motor, Hanwha, Lotte and HD Hyundai as clients — [한국경제 2026-08](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2026082371481)
- 마켓보로 (식봄 / 마켓봄, food-ingredient B2B): 2024 revenue 26.1 billion KRW. 2025 식봄 GMV was 234.1 billion KRW (+52%) and cumulative group GMV about 13 trillion KRW. Raised a 40 billion KRW Series C in 2022 (about 60 billion cumulative). Targets turning profitable in 2026 — [유니콘팩토리 2026-01](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2026010509120152226); [머니투데이 2025-04](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2025/04/02/2025040114343797309)

**팩토리온 FactoryOn (한국산업단지공단) – 공공**
- An online support system for setting up factories: site and permit pre-diagnosis, location recommendation and registered-factory data. The 3D analysis upgrade was announced 2026-11-03. It is not a transaction or matching marketplace, but its "전국등록공장현황" (nationwide registered-factory list) open data is a usable supplier database — [산업통상부](https://www.motir.go.kr/kor/article/ATCL8764a1224/155119253/view); [공공데이터포털](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15131266/fileData.do)

### Inferences
- The only profitable, scaled matchers at hundreds of billions of KRW in GMV (Kmong, Soomgo, and Wishket by volume) all handle services where the deliverable is digital or labor. They work because escrow plus a manager-led "enterprise" layer is enough to build trust. Physical manufacturing matchers (CAPA, O-SL) remain small: partner counts in the low thousands and revenue in the low billions of KRW at best.
- "Enterprise" managed-service tiers (Kmong Enterprise, Soomgo Bizlink) look like the main growth lever: Kmong's enterprise business grew more than 56%. Managed matching, rather than self-serve listing, seems to be what B2B buyers pay for.
- MRO giants (iMarketKorea, Serveone) handle catalogue procurement for conglomerates. They are not a good fit for custom one-off work such as packaging or crating, but they own the buyer relationships.

### Gaps
- Fee rates (수수료) for CAPA, Wishket, Kmong Enterprise and Bizlink were not confirmed from official pages.
- Not researched or no data found: 나라장터 (KONEPS) scale and fees, 하이웨어 (Hiware), smart-factory platforms, 바로고, 로지스밸리, 메이커스 (Makers), and cosmetics/food OEM matching platforms. I found no dedicated cosmetics OEM matching startup with disclosed funding; searches returned only OEM manufacturers themselves (코디, 리봄화장품) on [THE VC](https://thevc.kr/smart-collections/689c2e8f8f9f962863d3fd01).
- THE VC paid financials and DART were inaccessible, so CAPA, Wishket and Packative revenue is unverified.

## 2. 실패·피벗·종료 사례

### Takeaway
B2B and vertical platforms in Korea have closed in large numbers since 2022 because they could not raise follow-on funding. Notable cases near this space are Linkshops (Dongdaemun wholesale), Urbanbase (B2B SaaS) and Kmong's "쑨". Fassto survived by pivoting away from pure fulfillment toward robotics and by dropping unprofitable clients.

### Cited Findings
- Linkshops closed after full capital impairment despite raising 16.5 billion KRW. The article frames this as part of a wave of vertical platforms closing over funding shortages — [한국경제 2025-03](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025032738971)
- In 1H 2024, 68 VC-backed startups closed, and 38 of them (56%) were platform businesses — [한국경제 2024-07](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2024073128801)
- Urbanbase (3D interior, B2B SaaS, once valued at about 400 billion KRW) closed in July 2024 — [나무위키](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%96%B4%EB%B0%98%EB%B2%A0%EC%9D%B4%EC%8A%A4) (secondary source)
- Services discontinued in 2024 include Kmong's "쑨" (short-term job matching) — [Outstanding](https://www.threads.com/@outstanding_crew/post/DEixXZpSbFJ)
- In 2022, 오늘회, 힙합퍼 and 부릉 suspended services or were sold because of a cash crunch — [경향신문](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202210311605001)
- Fassto pivoted to 파스토로보틱스 and RaaS, cut loss-making clients, and missed its Series D target (19 billion raised against 50 billion sought) — [Byline](https://byline.network/2026/02/20_fassto/); [디지털데일리](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2024090210093746097)

### Inferences
- Likely common failure modes: thin take rates on low-frequency custom orders, disintermediation once buyer and supplier meet (repeat orders move offline), and high cost of onboarding offline suppliers. These are inferred from the cases above, not stated explicitly in the sources.

### Gaps
- I found no documented closure of a manufacturing-specific matching platform (for example a "공장 매칭" startup), only general platform closures. Logiket's current status is unknown.

## 3. 남은 공백 (verticals, 신뢰 검증, 오프라인 산업)

### Takeaway
Industrial packaging (wooden crates, pallets, export packaging) has no visible online quote or matching platform in Korea. Search returned only regulatory guides (IPPC/ISPM-15) and pallet pooling (LogisAll/KPP rental). Trust tools in existing platforms are mostly escrow and reviews. None publicly offers on-site factory inspection or capability verification at scale. CAPA's "30만 공장" claim, set against roughly 2,000–2,700 actual partners, shows the gap between a directory and a vetted supplier network.

### Cited Findings
- For export wood packaging, a search turned up only IPPC-mark and fumigation guidance plus pallet pooling. I found no online quote platform for 목포장 (wooden crating) or 산업포장 (industrial packaging) — [Tradlinx](https://www.tradlinx.com/blog/guide/ippc-mark/); [LogisAll KPP](https://klp.logisall.com/en/sub/business/new/logisticsRecovery.asp)
- Risks in this vertical: shipments rejected at the destination for untreated wood, and fines in Korea for non-compliant packaging. This makes certification checking a real trust need — [Tradlinx](https://www.tradlinx.com/blog/guide/%EB%AA%A9%EC%9E%AC%ED%8C%94%EB%A0%88%ED%8A%B8-ippc-mark-%ED%91%9C%EA%B8%B0-%ED%99%95%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EC%85%A8%EB%82%98%EC%9A%94/)
- Packative serves branded retail boxes (paper and corrugated, small minimum orders), not heavy industrial packaging — [Packative FAQ](https://packative.com/help/customer-center/frequently-asked)
- Trust tools in use: Wishket requires escrow deposit before work starts; Soomgo Bizlink uses escrow plus a dedicated manager; Kmong Enterprise uses manager recommendations — [머니투데이](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2022/10/29/2022102817271714123); [넥스트유니콘](https://www.nextunicorn.kr/content/154eca04bc012215); [Kmong Enterprise](https://kmong.com/enterprise)
- User complaints about weak dispute resolution on Wishket (anecdotal) — [Clien](https://www.clien.net/service/board/cm_app/18549893)
- 3PL review sites score providers on SLA accuracy, communication, system integration and contract transparency. This suggests buyers want performance-based verification, not just listings — [sellernow](https://sellernow.co.kr/post/497609)
- Public supplier data exists (nationwide registered-factory list) but is not transactional — [공공데이터포털](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15131266/fileData.do)

### Inferences
- Gaps that look open: (1) industrial and export packaging (crating, pallets, ISPM-15 heat treatment); (2) offline-heavy processing such as plating, painting, heat treatment and assembly subcontracting, where suppliers are not digitized; (3) verified capability data (equipment, certifications, past delivery performance) rather than self-reported profiles; (4) post-match tools (quality inspection, delivery tracking) that discourage taking repeat orders offline.
- Government data such as the FactoryOn registered-factory list could seed a supplier database at low cost.

### Gaps
- No market-size data on 산업포장 or 목포장 in Korea was found in this pass.
- I found no source on how many listed suppliers are actually verified on any platform.

## 4. 최근(2024–2026) 진입자 및 투자

### Takeaway
I found few clearly identifiable 2024–2026 funding rounds for Korean B2B manufacturing matchers. The most concrete recent items are Packative's 2026 Deep-tech TIPS (AI quoting for packaging makers), the government's 2026 "온라인제조플랫폼서비스" program recruiting platform companies, Kmong's 2025 profitability, and MarketBoro's growth. Capital appears to be moving toward AI manufacturing tools rather than pure marketplaces.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 government program "디자인-온라인제조플랫폼서비스": it recruits platform companies that match demand firms with suppliers (OEM/ODM, idle-capacity factories) for prototype development. This is a public subsidy channel for matchers — [THE VC 지원사업 공고 PDF](https://grant-documents.thevc.kr/291373_2026%EB%85%84+%EB%94%94%EC%9E%90%EC%9D%B8-%EC%98%A8%EB%9D%BC%EC%9D%B8%EC%A0%9C%EC%A1%B0%ED%94%8C%EB%9E%AB%ED%8F%BC%EC%84%9C%EB%B9%84%EC%8A%A4+%EC%82%AC%EC%97%85+%ED%94%8C%EB%9E%AB%ED%8F%BC%EA%B8%B0%EC%97%85+%EB%AA%A8%EC%A7%91%EA%B3%B5%EA%B3%A0.pdf)
- Packative (프로보티브): 2026 Deep-tech TIPS, building AI for packaging manufacturers' design, quoting and order intake, Series A planned — [VentureSquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/?p=1123182)
- 모노스케일 (Monoscale): listed as a regional manufacturer-matching platform; funding amount not disclosed — [THE VC](https://thevc.kr/monosacle/products)
- Adjacent 2025 manufacturing-AI rounds (not matchers): 로아이 raised a 1.4 billion KRW seed for an AI robotics autonomous-production solution — [VentureSquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/1091449/)
- 2026 Korean VC market outlook: about 13–15 trillion KRW total, with AI and deep-tech policy funds — [Brunch venture-digest](https://brunch.co.kr/@venture-digest/17) (blog-level source)

### Inferences
- A new entrant would likely compete for TIPS and government platform grants rather than large VC rounds, unless it frames itself as AI-enabled (for example automated quoting).

### Gaps
- I found no confirmed 2024–2026 rounds for CAPA, O-SL, Wishket or Logiket. The 머니투데이 2026-01 article on 비링커 (founder's father's factory, revenue up 10x) could not be confirmed as a matching business — [머니투데이](https://www.mt.co.kr/article/2026012014190015944).
