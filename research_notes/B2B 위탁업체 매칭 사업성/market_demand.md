## 1. Official statistics: how many companies outsource or subcontract, transaction volume, and SME dependence

### Takeaway
About 1 in 6 Korean SMEs (excluding 소상공인) is a 수급(수탁)기업. In 2024 these firms had 584조원 in sales, and 67.3% of it (393조원) came from sales to their 위탁기업. In manufacturing the dependence is 72.5%. On the large-company side, the 공시대상기업집단 alone pay about 89조원 in 하도급대금 every half-year (about 178조원 a year). This is a large, structural market, but it consists mostly of fixed 원청–하청 relationships, not open buyer–supplier discovery.

### Cited Findings
- **2024년 기준 중소기업실태조사 (MSS, published 2026-01-09):** SMEs excluding 소상공인 had total sales of 2,085조원 and 792만 employees. — [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000283667); [트윅24](https://www.twig24.com/news/2026/01/09/20260109500039)
- **Share of 수급기업:** 16.7% of all SMEs are 수급기업. Their total sales were 584조원, of which 393조원 came from transactions with 위탁기업, so their 위탁기업 의존도 is 67.3%. — [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000283667)
- **By sector:** Manufacturing has the largest share of 수급기업, both by firm count (47.9%) and by sales (43.3%). Manufacturing 수급기업 depend on 위탁기업 for 72.5% of sales. Manufacturing SMEs had sales of 638조원 (30.6% of SME sales) and 193.1만 employees (24.4%). Wholesale and retail had sales of 649조원 (31.1%). — [전자신문](https://www.etnews.com/20260109000137); [세계일보](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260109505471)
- **Main pain point in 수·위탁거래:** The most cited problem is that raw-material price increases are not reflected in 납품단가 (38.6%). — [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000283667)

### Inferences
- **Size of the dependent group:** 16.7% of SMEs being 수급기업 points to roughly tens of thousands of non-micro manufacturers and service firms whose revenue depends on 위탁 relationships. The exact count is not in the snippets.
- **Market scale:** 393조원 a year of 수급기업 sales to 위탁기업 marks the upper bound of the transaction pool. Chaebol 하도급 alone is about 180조원 a year.
- **Where a platform fits:** 67–72% revenue dependence on existing 위탁기업 means most supply relationships are locked in. A discovery and verification platform is more likely to win in three areas:
  - (a) 수탁기업 looking to diversify customers away from one dominant buyer;
  - (b) SMEs and startups that are buyers themselves and have no 협력사 pool;
  - (c) non-core categories such as packaging, logistics and staffing.
- **Trust as a platform feature:** About 17% of surveyed 위탁기업 were suspected of 상생협력법 violations, and 92억원 of unpaid 대금 was recovered in one sweep. Payment security (escrow or payment guarantees) and verification of a counterparty's reputation are therefore credible value propositions.

### Gaps
- The exact count of 수탁기업 and 위탁기업 in 2024 was not visible in the snippets. The MSS 통계DB page could not be fetched.
- The breakdown of 하도급 분쟁조정 cases in FTC/KOFAIR statistics (see Q3) was not found.

### Takeaway
Hard figures exist for corrugated packaging (about 5.2조원 in 2022), cosmetics OEM/ODM (about 10조원 in 2024), 뿌리산업 (about 64,000 firms and 74만 workers at the end of 2023) and outsourced machining (about 22조원, a startup's estimate). No reliable figures were found for wooden crating, pallets or staffing, and 3PL figures come only from foreign research firms.

- **Staffing compliance as a feature:** Illegal dispatch through unlicensed 인력공급업체 is a documented problem. A platform that verifies dispatch licenses would address a real risk for buyers.

## 3. How Korean companies find and vet suppliers, and their pain points

### Takeaway
No Korea-wide survey was found that shows supplier-discovery channels as percentages (지인 소개, 검색, 전시회 and so on). Nearby evidence does show that discovery is a top pain point (70.3% of trade SMEs cite buyer and partner discovery). Disputes are also rising: KOFAIR's 공정거래 분쟁조정 intake hit a record 4,726 cases in 2025. Payment risk is concrete: 92억원 of unpaid 납품대금 surfaced in one MSS inspection, and about 1 in 4 construction subs did not receive full payment.

### Cited Findings
- **Discovery difficulty:** In a 2026 KITA survey of 1,090 trade firms (93.2% SMEs), 70.3% named buyer and partner discovery (바이어·거래선 발굴) as their biggest difficulty. The most cited benefit of support programs was new-buyer discovery (54.6%). — [헤럴드경제](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10879858); [KITA](https://www.kita.net/board/pressData/pressDataDetail.do?no=12205)
- **KITA's channel strategy:** KITA plans to link offline touchpoints (exhibitions, 상담회) with online channels such as tradeKorea. This shows the current channel mix is still exhibition-heavy. — [헤럴드경제](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10879858)
- **KOFAIR (한국공정거래조정원) dispute mediation:**
  - Intake: 3,481 cases (2023), 4,041 (2024), 4,726 (2025, +17% and a record since 2008).
  - 2025 processed: 4,407 cases. Mediation succeeded in 1,709.
  - Platform-related disputes grew sharply, and about half of 2025 platform cases involved Coupang.
  - Sources: [헤럴드경제](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026051221444761690); [뉴스서울](https://newsseoul.co.kr/news/view/1065588018352173); [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260513_0003627532)
- **Unpaid 대금:**
  - The MSS inspection recovered 92억원 of unpaid 납품대금 from 479 firms. — [전자신문](https://m.etnews.com/20260730000118)
  - 24.3% of construction 수급사업자 did not receive full payment even under 직불합의. — [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260731_0003731274)
- **Pricing:** Failure to pass raw-material cost increases into 납품단가 is the top 수·위탁 pain point (38.6%, 2024 basis). — [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000283667)
- **Technology misappropriation (legal changes):**
  - From 2024-08-29, damages for 기술자료 유용 rose to up to 5x actual damages.
  - From 2025-12-17, 수급사업자 can ask a court directly to stop violations (하도급법 제34조의2).
  - Source: [법률신문](https://www.lawtimes.co.kr/news/articleViewAmp.html?idxno=214246); [로톡](https://www.lawtalk.co.kr/posts/138032)

### Inferences
- **Search costs are high:** Discovery is the top pain point even for exporters, who have strong institutional support from KITA and KOTRA. Domestic SMEs looking for niche suppliers (crating, machining) likely face similar or higher search costs. This is not directly measured.
- **Trust features have demand:** Disputes are rising and unpaid 대금 is documented. Verified business info (사업자 상태, 신용, 인증, 파견허가), reviews and payment protection address real, measurable risks.

### Gaps
- No percentage survey was found on domestic B2B supplier-sourcing channels (지인 소개, 검색, 전시회, 구매대행). A useful search target is the 중기중앙회 or KOSME (중진공) 판로 실태조사, where 기업실태조사 may include "거래처 확보 경로."
- The FTC/KOFAIR 하도급-specific split of the 4,726 cases was not found.
- The 2025 서면실태조사 figures for 대금 미지급 and 기술유용 were not found.
- No survey was found on supplier quality failures.

## 4. Trends: digital procurement, the MRO and 구매대행 market, and manufacturing digital transformation

### Takeaway
SME manufacturing is still early in going digital. Smart-factory adoption is 18.6% among SMEs, 75.5% of adopters are at a basic level, and manufacturing AI adoption is 0.1%. Meanwhile, chaebol-linked MRO and 구매대행 platforms are large (서브원 is expected to pass 6조원 in revenue in 2026), and startups are building manufacturing-matching platforms (CAPA, 비링커, the Cafe24 manufacturer-matching service).

### Cited Findings
- **First 스마트제조혁신 실태조사 (MSS, published 2025-04-28):**
  - Smart-factory adoption: 19.5% of all firms, 18.6% of SMEs.
  - 75.5% of adopters remain at a basic level.
  - Manufacturing AI adoption: 0.1%.
  - Sample: 5,000 firms, interviewed in person from October 2024 to January 2025.
  - Source: [디지털데일리](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2025042816294926205)
- **KBIZ survey of smart-factory participants (2025-10, 502 firms):**
  - 78% see AI adoption as necessary.
  - The biggest barrier is upfront cost (44%).
  - 72% want direct funding.
  - Source: [뉴시스](https://mobile.newsis.com/view/NISX20251017_0003367189)
- **Digital maturity:** A KBIZ digital-maturity survey of 414 firms scored SMEs 41.4/100. The survey date is unclear. — [KDI 경제정보센터 (via search)](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=266057&topic=P)
- **MRO and 구매대행:**
  - 서브원, the No. 1 Korean MRO firm, was projected in August 2026 to pass 6조원 in annual revenue for the first time in 2026. This is a forecast. — [한국경제](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2026082371481)
  - 아이마켓코리아 is the only KOSPI-listed 구매대행 company. Its platform is used by 72만 corporate employees, and it is building the 그래디언트 테크놀러지 파크 industrial complex (groundbreaking in November 2025). — [한국경제](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202506221361i)
  - Its 2025 revenue is reported inconsistently: about 1조9,221억원 on one job site versus 3조원+ on JobKorea. Verify in DART. — [캐치](https://www.catch.co.kr/Comp/RecruitInfo/156508); [잡코리아](https://www.jobkorea.co.kr/company/1794249/?C_IDX=228)
  - No data was found on 행복나래 (SK).
- **Manufacturing-matching startups:**
  - CAPA (에이팀벤처스): Series B of 50억원 in January 2023 from LX인터내셔널, 하나증권 and Altos. At that time it had about 2,200 partner manufacturers and about 1만 clients, had introduced payments as a revenue model, and was investing in AI-based automated quoting. — [바이라인네트워크](https://byline.network/2023/01/27_9397761/)
  - 비링커 takes turnkey orders and routes them to a network of 임가공, 금형 and 사출 shops. It claims up to 20% time and cost savings. — [머니투데이](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2023/10/22/2023102013401260790)
  - Cafe24 launched demand-based manufacturer matching. A seller posts requirements, and qualifying manufacturers send quotes with unit price, MOQ and lead time. — [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/126507/cafe24-launches-business-manufacturer-matching-service)

### Inferences
- **Low digital maturity cuts both ways:** It means friction in sourcing (an opportunity), but suppliers may be slow to onboard and keep profiles up to date. Supply-side onboarding will be a key cost.
- **Where the openings are:** MRO giants focus on indirect materials for large enterprises. CAPA and 비링커 focus on machining and parts. This leaves room for a cross-category platform covering packaging, 3PL, OEM and staffing for SMEs, but category-specific competitors already exist. Their traction since 2023 was not verified.

### Gaps
- No total size was found for Korea's MRO or 구매대행 market.
- No 2025–2026 traction figures were found for CAPA or 비링커.
- No statistic was found on Korean SMEs' e-procurement or B2B marketplace adoption rates.
